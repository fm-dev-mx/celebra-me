## Validation and migration boundary

For every row, the contract suite verifies prerequisites, non-origin portability, fail-closed
incompatible input, and CSS-owner resolution. The visual suite compares the variant at 390x844 and
1440x900 with a maximum pixel ratio of 0.001.

Migration of existing Local, Preview, or Production rows is separately authorized. A guarded
preflight must verify the current source hash, apply canonical content transactionally, and verify
schema, render contract, and parity before advancing to the next environment. Raw payloads,
credentials, record identifiers, and remote URLs do not belong in this document.
